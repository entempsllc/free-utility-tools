# Free Utility Tools — Project Brief

- **Project ID:** `free-utility-tools`
- **Product:** Small collection of free, browser-based utility tools
- **Live URL:** https://entempsllc.github.io/free-utility-tools/
- **Repository:** `/Users/jp/Projects/github-sites/free-utility-tools`

## Purpose and audience
Free Utility Tools serves developers, job seekers, freelancers, writers, and privacy-conscious users who need quick utility tasks completed without accounts or unnecessary uploads.

## Offering
Six free tools: privacy-first PDF merger, ATS resume keyword scanner, freelance rate calculator, word counter, password generator, and Base64 encoder/decoder. Most processing is presented as client-side.

## Content pillars
1. Privacy-conscious document and text utilities.
2. Developer encoding and security helpers.
3. Career and freelance workflow tools.
4. Clear navigation, truthful claims, and measurable tool usefulness.

## Monetization and boundaries
Potential paths are AdSense after content/traffic/account readiness, legitimate contextual affiliates where a real user need and approved program exist, or a narrowly validated digital product/API extension. AdSense script presence and placeholders do not prove approval, active units, traffic, RPM, or revenue. No affiliate or paid product path is currently verified. Option B allows tested, reversible low-risk publication but requires approval for indexing controls, outreach, spending, account/permission changes, pricing/payment changes, legal-policy changes, and deletions.
