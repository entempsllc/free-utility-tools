# Marketing Campaign: Free Utility Tools

## The Hook
A 100% client-side collection of everyday tools (Resume Scanners, PDF Mergers) that run directly in your browser and never upload your data.

## The Story
I was tired of uploading my sensitive tax documents and resumes to random "free" websites just to merge a PDF or check an ATS score. So I built a suite of offline-first, client-side tools using WebAssembly and local processing. No tracking, no uploads, completely free.

## Target URLs
* Hub: `https://entempsllc.github.io/free-utility-tools/`
* Resume Scanner: `https://entempsllc.github.io/free-utility-tools/resume-scanner/`
* PDF Merger: `https://entempsllc.github.io/free-utility-tools/pdf-tools/`

## 1. Hacker News (Show HN)

**Title:** Show HN: A suite of 100% client-side utility tools (PDF merger, ATS scanner)

**Body:**
Hey HN,

I noticed that a lot of standard online utility tools (like PDF mergers or resume scanners) require uploading files to third-party servers. For sensitive documents, this is an obvious privacy risk.

I built a collection of simple, client-side tools that do this directly in the browser. 
- The PDF tool uses WebAssembly to merge files locally.
- The ATS Resume Scanner extracts keywords and scores your resume locally without saving your data.

It's completely free and open source. Let me know what you think or if you'd like to see any other tools added!

URL: https://entempsllc.github.io/free-utility-tools/
