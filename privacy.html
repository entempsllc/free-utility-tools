Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

At Free Utility Tools, we prioritize your privacy. This policy explains how we handle data when you use our browser-based utilities.

1. 100% Client-Side Processing

Most of our tools—including the PDF Merger, Password Generator, Word Counter, and Base64 Tool—perform all calculations and processing directly in your web browser. Your documents, text, and passwords never leave your computer. We do not upload your files to any server for processing.

2. Data Collection

We do not require account registration or collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number to use our tools.

3. Cookies and Advertising

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings.

4. Analytics

We may use basic, privacy-preserving analytics to understand aggregate tool usage (e.g., how many times a tool was opened) to help us improve the service. We do not track individual users or the content you process within the tools.

5. External Links

Our site may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites.
